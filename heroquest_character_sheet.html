Character Sheet

A printable card-sized sheet for heroes and monsters that saves in your browser and exports to a file.

Fill in a hero (or switch to monster mode for a named villain or custom monster), give it a name, and it saves automatically in this browser. Choosing the Barbarian, Dwarf, Elf or Wizard fills in their starting stats. For pack heroes such as the Knight or the Druid, copy the numbers from the hero's character card. Print up to four cards per page.
