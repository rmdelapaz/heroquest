How the Combat Dice Work

HeroQuest's combat dice are six-sided, with three different symbols on them: three skulls, two white shields and one black shield.

  • The attacker rolls their attack dice. Every skull is a hit.
  • The defender rolls their defend dice. A hero blocks one hit for every white shield; a monster blocks one hit for every black shield.
  • Each hit that isn't blocked costs the defender one Body Point. The common monsters (Goblins, Orcs, Skeletons and Zombies) have only one Body Point, so a single unblocked skull kills them.

So a skull comes up half the time, a hero blocks with a third of their dice, and a monster blocks with only a sixth. That gap is why heroes can wade through Goblins and why every die of armour matters to them.

Movement Roller

Heroes roll two red six-sided dice for movement and may move up to the total in squares. Monsters don't roll: each one has a fixed Movement on its card.

Try It at the Table

  • Before a fight, set the roller to your hero against the monster in front of you and read the "kill within" line. It tells you whether to attack now or wait for a friend.
  • Set the defender to Hero and compare one defend die against two. That is what a helmet or a shield is worth to you.
  • Movement averages 7. If the door you need is 9 squares away, the table shows you have about a 28% chance of reaching it this turn.
