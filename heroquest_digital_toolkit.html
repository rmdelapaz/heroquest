Zargon's Toolkit

Trackers for the Zargon player: hero Body and Mind Points, monsters on the board, spells used this quest, the treasure deck, and campaign progress.

These trackers sit beside the real board; they don't replace the cards or the Game Master's screen. Everything saves automatically in this browser only (nothing is sent anywhere). Need dice? Use the Dice Roller.

❤️ Heroes: Body and Mind

Pick a hero to fill in the starting Body and Mind Points of the four core heroes. For pack heroes and custom heroes, choose "Other" and type the numbers from the character card.

👹 Monsters on the Board

Track damage on monsters with more than 1 Body Point. Picking a monster fills in its Body from the classic 1990 North American chart; check your Game Master's screen and change the number if yours differs.

🔮 Spells Used This Quest

Each hero spell can be cast once per quest, then it's gone until the quest ends. Tick a spell when it's cast. The spell names are the classic ones; check them against your own cards.

💰 Treasure Deck

When a hero searches a room for treasure, first check the quest notes for special treasure (only the first hero to search that room finds it). If there is none, the hero shuffles the treasure deck and draws a card.

  • Gold or a potion: the hero records it and keeps the card.
  • Hazard: do what the card says, then return it to the bottom of the deck.
  • Wandering monster: place the monster named in the quest notes next to the hero, roll its Attack dice at once, and return the card to the bottom of the deck.

Forgot the deck, or playing remotely? This virtual deck follows those rules. Set the counts to match your own cards before you start.

📜 Campaign Progress

The 2021 core Quest Book has fourteen quests. Tick each one the heroes complete; finishing all fourteen makes them Sovereign Knights.

💾 Saving

The toolkit saves after every change, in this browser on this device. Clearing your browser data erases it.
