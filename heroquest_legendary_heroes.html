The Heroes

Who the twelve official HeroQuest heroes are, which product each comes from, and the backstory the rulebook gives them.

Twelve Official Heroes

The HeroQuest Game System box comes with four heroes: the Barbarian, Dwarf, Elf and Wizard. Since 2021 Avalon Hill has added eight more through hero collections and quest packs: the Knight, Rogue, Monk, Warlock, Bard, Druid, Berserker and Explorer. Each has its own page in this guide; this page is the overview.

Where Each Hero Comes From

Classic edition (1989–1990): the original Milton Bradley and Games Workshop game had only the four core heroes. The early-1990s North American quest packs The Frozen Horror and The Mage of the Mirror added a female Barbarian and a female Elf figure, but these were new figures for the existing heroes, not new heroes.

The Story Behind the Heroes

The 2021 rulebook opens with Mentor, the guardian of the Loretome, telling of an earlier band of heroes. Their leader was Rogar, a barbarian prince who bore the Star of the West. With him were Durgin the dwarf, Ladril, an elven fighter-mage from Elethorn, and Telor, a wizard. The four core heroes match that company: a barbarian, a dwarf, an elf and a wizard.

The enemy is Zargon, once Mentor's apprentice, who pledged himself to the powers of Dread. In the core quests the new heroes prove themselves in Quest 1, "The Trial", then serve the King: they rescue Sir Ragnar, one of the King's knights, collect rewards from Prince Magnus, and face Ulag the Orc Warlord and later his son Grak. Heroes who complete all fourteen quests become Sovereign Knights.

Classic edition (1989–1990): in the 1989 UK and European edition the villain is called Morcar, and the story speaks of the Emperor rather than the King. The 1990 North American edition renamed Morcar to Zargon, and the 2021 game keeps Zargon.

For more on Mentor, Rogar and the people who send the heroes on quests, see the lore page Mentors & Questgivers.

The Core Four

These four come in the HeroQuest Game System box and in the 2025 starter set HeroQuest: First Light, which has the same rules. Their starting stats are the same as in the original game.

The Quest-Pack Heroes

Each of these heroes arrived with a specific product. Their rules come on their own hero cards, so the details below stick to what each product is confirmed to add; the hero pages go further.

The Knight

From: Commander of the Guardian Knights, a hero collection first sold as a limited preorder in late 2021 and shipped in 2022. It sold out in about a day and the original figure can't be produced again. Knight figures are also included in the quest pack Rise of the Dread Moon (2023).

The Rogue

From: The Rogue Heir of Elethorn, a hero collection shipped in December 2022 with two figures. The Rogue is an elf, from the same Elethorn that Ladril came from in the backstory.

The Monk

From: The Path of the Wandering Monk, a hero collection from 2023 that reached wider retail in early 2024, with two figures. The Monk is human and fights unarmed, drawing on elemental power.

The Warlock

From: the quest pack The Prophecy of Telor (13 quests), one of the packs unlocked during the 2020 HasLab crowdfunding campaign. The Warlock's signature ability is Demonform, which adds an attack die and lets them ignore traps. One product list describes the Warlock as a halfling.

The Bard

From: the quest pack Spirit Queen's Torment (14 quests), also a HasLab stretch goal. The Bard is an orc who fights with a rapier that can attack diagonally and sings songs such as Lullaby, Healing Song and Inspiring Tale.

The Druid

From: the quest pack Against the Ogre Horde (2024), with male and female figures. The Druid brings an Animal Ally, a wolf companion.

The Berserker and the Explorer

From: the quest pack Jungles of Delthrak (2024, 16 quests with branching paths), each in male and female versions. The Berserker is human and the Explorer is a dwarf. The pack also includes Raptor and Saberfang companions.

New Figures, Same Heroes

Several products add a new figure for an existing hero rather than a new hero:

  • The Frozen Horror (2022): an alternate Barbarian.
  • The Mage of the Mirror (2023): an alternate Elf, who starts the pack's quests alone.
  • Wizards of Morcar (March 2026): an alternate Wizard with the same stats as the core Wizard.
  • HeroQuest: First Light (2025): new figures for all four core heroes.

The 2021 art shows male and female versions of the four core heroes. Which versions come as figures depends on the box you buy.

Not Official Heroes

You'll find a Sorcerer, Necromancer, Paladin, Ranger, Cleric or Thief on fan sites, but none of them is an official HeroQuest hero. The Necromancer appears in Wizards of Morcar as one of Zargon's sorcerers, an enemy. Fan-made heroes can be fun; Custom Heroes & Monsters covers how to build one that fits the game.
