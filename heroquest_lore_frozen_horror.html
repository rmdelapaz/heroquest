The Frozen Horror: Lore

The story of The Frozen Horror as the quest books tell it, with a clearly labelled fan elaboration of the frozen north and the creature beneath the ice.

The quest books sketch this story only briefly, so this page mixes official names from the books with fan elaboration, and labels which is which.

"North of the last farms the snow stops melting. North of the last forts the maps stop being maps. Somewhere beyond that, under the ice, something waits. The people who live up there do not name it. The heroes will learn to call it the Frozen Horror..."

What the Quest Books Establish

  • The Frozen Horror is a creature. It is the monster the pack is named for, and it comes as its own miniature. Beyond that, this guide does not invent a history for it.
  • The Barbarian leads. The pack centres on the Barbarian and includes an alternate Barbarian figure.
  • The cold has teeth. The pack's monsters are Ice Gremlins, Polar Warbears and Yetis, none of which appear in the core box.
  • The heroes hire help. Twelve Mercenaries come in the box: paid fighters who march with the heroes into the north.
  • The world around it. In the core 2021 books the enemy is Zargon and his magic is Dread. The core Quest Book also names the Northern Dread Wastes, though how the pack's frozen lands relate to them is for the quest book to say.

Classic edition (1989–1990): the original Frozen Horror was a North America-only "Barbarian Quest Pack" from the early 1990s. It came with a female Barbarian figure and opened with the Barbarian questing alone before the rest of the party joined, and it introduced mercenaries and ice monsters. It was never released in Europe, so there is no Morcar-era version of this story.

A Fan's Telling: The Cold That Asks You to Stop

Fan elaboration. Nothing in this section is from the quest books; use it as table colour if you like it.

Most evils in the world want something: gold, power, the King's throne. The thing under the ice seems to want nothing at all except stillness. Travellers who come back from the north talk less about claws and more about the quiet: no birds, no wind in the right places, footprints that stop.

The creatures that live near it are hungry rather than wicked. Ice Gremlins pick at the edges of a camp. Polar Warbears and Yetis are the north's own giants, and something has made them bolder than they should be. A party that marches north with only four swords soon learns why the frontier villages hire every blade they can find, and why the Barbarian, raised to this kind of cold, walks at the front.

That is the Horror's real weapon in this telling: not the cold itself, but the voice in it that says the march is too long, the north too wide, and that it would be so easy to stop and rest. Heroes who keep walking are the ones who reach it.

Using This Story at the Table

As Zargon, let the cold live in your descriptions, not in extra rules: the pack has no temperature or hypothermia track, and the heroes fight with the normal rules. Give the hired mercenaries a line or two of personality when the heroes recruit them; it makes losing one in a corridor matter. Read each quest's introduction for the official story.
