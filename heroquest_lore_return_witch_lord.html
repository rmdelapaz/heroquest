Return of the Witch Lord: Lore

The story of the Witch Lord's return as the quest books tell it, with a clearly labelled fan elaboration of the dead lord of Barak Tor.

The quest books sketch this story only briefly, so this page mixes official names from the books with fan elaboration, and labels which is which.

"Death, they say, is the great equalizer, the one border no amount of power can cross. The Witch Lord never accepted that. He was put down once, with the only blade that could do it, in the barrow where he had slept for an age. Now the barrow is quiet, and the quiet is wrong..."

What the Quest Books Establish

  • Barak Tor. The Witch Lord's barrow. The core Quest Book's twelfth quest is "Barak Tor—Barrow of the Witch Lord".
  • The Spirit Blade. The heroes go looking for it in core Quest 13, "Quest for the Spirit Blade", and carry it back in Quest 14, "Return to Barak Tor". Heroes who complete all fourteen core quests become Sovereign Knights.
  • His return. As the pack's title says, the Witch Lord comes back. The 2021 pack is built around his undead: skeletons, zombies and mummies.
  • Who stands behind the dark. In the 2021 books the great enemy is Zargon, Mentor's former apprentice, and his magic is Dread magic. The heroes serve the realm and its King.

Classic edition (1989–1990): the original Return of the Witch Lord came out in 1989 in Europe and 1991 in North America, with the same undead theme. Its European text names the villain Morcar and speaks of the Emperor rather than the King. The North American books use Zargon and added Chaos spells, which the 2021 game renames Dread spells; the 2021 books use Zargon, the King and Dread.

A Fan's Telling: The Lord Who Would Not Stay Dead

Fan elaboration. Nothing in this section is from the quest books; use it as table colour if you like it.

The Witch Lord's strength was never his sword arm. It was his refusal. When the Spirit Blade struck him down, something of that refusal stayed behind in the stones of Barak Tor, and in every grave and crypt within reach of it.

His servants are the oldest kind. Skeletons that remember how to hold a blade and nothing else. Zombies, slow and patient, that will follow a living scent down any corridor. Mummies, bound in old cloth and older magic, harder to put down than anything else in his halls. None of them fear. None of them tire. They simply come, and keep coming, until the heroes find the will behind them.

The heroes who face him again know one thing the first time did not teach: they have beaten him before. That is the whole of their advantage, and it has to be enough.

Using This Story at the Table

As Zargon, remind the players of the core book's ending before the first quest: they carried the Spirit Blade back to Barak Tor to finish the Witch Lord, and now he is back. That memory is the hook. Read each quest's own introduction for the official story, and keep the undead quiet until they move; a room of still skeletons is more unsettling than a shouted threat.
