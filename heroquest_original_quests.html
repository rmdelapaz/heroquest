The Core Quest Book

The 14 quests of the 2021 HeroQuest Quest Book in order, with each quest's goal, reward and what it asks of the heroes.

About the Quest Book

The 2021 HeroQuest Game System comes with a Quest Book of 14 quests, meant to be played in order as one campaign. Each quest is a single page: a map, a parchment text from Mentor that Zargon reads aloud, and notes that only Zargon sees. Heroes keep their gold, equipment and treasure from one quest to the next.

  • A quest ends when the heroes complete the goal and get back to the stairway (or the exit the quest names). If all four heroes die, the quest is lost.
  • The stories link up. Ulag the Orc Warlord runs through Quests 2, 3 and 6, and the Witch Lord through Quests 12 to 14.
  • The prize: heroes who complete all 14 quests become Sovereign Knights.

Playing the Campaign

  • The first three quests are your bank. The rewards for Quests 2 and 3 add up to 420 gold for the party. Decide early whether to pool it for one big purchase (say, a longsword for the Dwarf or Elf) or spread it out. See Treasure & Equipment.
  • Redraft spells each quest. The Elf and Wizard choose schools before every quest, so read the parchment first. Quest 8's Balur, immune to fire, is the obvious case.
  • Escapes test movement. Quests 6 and 9 are about getting out, not fighting everything. Averages matter: two red dice give 7 squares, but only 41.7% of rolls reach 8 or more.
  • The last three are one story. Plan Quests 12 to 14 together, and think about who will carry the Spirit Blade.

Classic edition (1989–1990): the original Quest Book has the same 14-quest campaign, but the 1989 European edition and the 1990 North American edition differ from each other and from 2021. The European book names the villain Morcar and the realm's ruler the Emperor, uses Fimir and Chaos Warriors, and gives every monster 1 Body Point; its Quest 7 is "The Stone Hunter" rather than "The Lost Wizard". The North American edition renamed the villain Zargon, made the monsters tougher and added more monsters and traps to the quests. Quest 11 was "Bastion of Chaos" in both. The 2021 book follows the North American version with the Dread renames.

Try It at the Table

  • Before each quest, read the parchment together and agree on the goal in one sentence. "Bring Ragnar back alive" and "kill everything" call for very different play.
  • After each quest, note on your character sheets what you earned and spent. By Quest 12 you'll see which purchases actually mattered.
  • If you're Zargon for the first time, run The Trial straight from the book, then read Playing Zargon before Quest 2.
